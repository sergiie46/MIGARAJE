# Google Play — English

**Name:** Mi Garaje

**Short description:** Track maintenance, expenses, fuel economy and the full history of your vehicles.

**Full description:**
Mi Garaje keeps the full life of your vehicles in one place: mileage, maintenance, repairs, refuels, expenses, tyres, inspections, insurance, taxes, parts and modifications.

Track fuel fills and calculate fuel economy and running cost when enough full-to-full data is available. Schedule maintenance by date or mileage, receive reminders, search your history and review expenses by category and period.

Enthusiasts can keep a detailed modification log covering engine, turbo, intake, exhaust, electronics, suspension, brakes, drivetrain, differential, wheels, interior, exterior, lighting and audio, including total project cost.

Your data can be synchronized through your Google/Firebase account and restored on another device. Offline Firestore caching keeps previously loaded information available without a connection.

The FREE tier supports up to 2 active vehicles and may show discreet ads. Mi Garaje PRO is a one-time purchase that removes ads, unlocks unlimited vehicles and enables PDF/CSV exports and advanced features.
